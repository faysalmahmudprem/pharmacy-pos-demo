# Pharmacy POS (Point of Sale) System Report

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Pharmacy POS
**Platform:** Web-based Application
**Framework:** Laravel 10.x
**Description:** A comprehensive Point of Sale and Inventory Management system tailored for pharmacies. It handles sales processing, medicine inventory tracking, customer relationships, and financial transactions (payments/dues).

## Technology Stack
- **Backend:** PHP 8.1+, Laravel 10
- **Frontend:** Vite, Axios, Blade Templates, CSS/JS
- **Database:** Relational Database (MySQL/SQLite/PostgreSQL supported by Laravel)
- **PDF Generation:** barryvdh/laravel-dompdf
- **Development Tools:** Laravel Sail, Pint, Tinker

## Key Features
1. **Dashboard:** Real-time overview of business performance (assumed from DashboardController).
2. **Point of Sale (POS):** 
   - Interactive interface to process sales.
   - Real-time stock updates.
   - Automatic invoice generation.
3. **Inventory Management (Medicines):**
   - CRUD operations for medicines.
   - Tracking by Name, Generic Name, Brand, Category, and Barcode.
   - Stock and price management (Purchase vs Sell price).
   - Activity status (Active/Inactive).
4. **Customer Management:**
   - Database of customers with contact info.
   - Tracking of total dues per customer.
   - Detailed payment history for each customer.
5. **Invoice System:**
   - Digital invoice viewing.
   - Export invoices to PDF format for printing.
6. **Sales Tracking:**
   - Detailed sale records including items, totals, paid amounts, and dues.
   - Invoice numbering.

## Database Schema (Core Models)
- **User:** Authentication and administration.
- **Medicine:** name, generic_name, brand, category, barcode, purchase_price, sell_price, stock, is_active.
- **Customer:** name, phone, total_due.
- **Sale:** customer_id, invoice_no, total, paid, due.
- **SaleItem:** sale_id, medicine_id, quantity, unit_price, subtotal.
- **Payment:** customer_id, amount, note.
- **Batch:** Placeholder for future batch-wise tracking features.

## Architecture
- **Controllers:** Organized by domain (POS, Sale, Medicine, Customer, Dashboard, Invoice).
- **Routes:** Structured RESTful and action-based web routes.
- **Seeders:** Includes a robust `MedicineSeeder` with over 100 common Bangladeshi pharmacy medicine brands and categories.
- **Migrations:** Evolutionary database schema with field updates for customers and sales.

## Testing & Validation
- **Feature Tests:**
  - `SaleFlowTest`: Validates the end-to-end process of creating a sale and its impact on inventory/accounts.
  - `CustomerPaymentTest`: Verifies customer payment processing and due updates.

## Current Status
The system is functional with core POS, Inventory, and Customer features implemented. It includes a rich set of seed data for quick deployment and testing.
